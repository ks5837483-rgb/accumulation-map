CANONICAL EXTRACTION PROMPT — mechanism-middle extraction (schema mechanism-triples-1.4)
Prompt of record for the out_mechanism/ layer (5_mechanism/out_mechanism/). Placeholders in {BRACES}.
The substantive rules live in mechanism_spec.txt; this wrapper only points the model at the spec + the paper.

---
You recover the UNMEASURED MECHANISM MIDDLE of each hypothesis in ONE paper, from your own careful FULL
reading. Faithfulness to the paper's OWN words and OWN signs is the entire point — you are not improving
the paper's logic, you are transcribing its causal chain.

1. Read the spec: {SPEC} — follow it EXACTLY. In one breath: per hypothesis emit a strict 2-step chain
   IV --[sign1]--> MECHANISM --[sign2]--> DV; relation is ONLY increases|decreases; the MECHANISM is the
   unmeasured mediating construct, named in the paper's OWN wording and NEVER flipped to its opposite
   (information_asymmetry stays information_asymmetry — the first edge MAY be "decreases"); EVERY edge and
   EVERY mechanism carries a VERBATIM evidence_span (copy exactly, never paraphrase — no quote, no edge);
   moderation goes in the "moderators" field, NEVER as a fabricated slope node; compute net = sign1 x sign2
   and VALIDATE it against the hypothesis's stated_direction, FLAGGING conflicts (never force a sign, never
   drop a record); AND per hypothesis record the TESTED outcome support_status (supported|rejected|mixed|
   not_reported) with a VERBATIM results-sentence support_evidence_span (rule 12) — the DATA's verdict, read
   from the results and kept INDEPENDENT of the predicted direction; classify each MODERATOR's role
   (mechanism_probe|competing_contingency|atheoretical) + is_boundary_condition (rule 13); and per hypothesis
   record alternatives_addressed (yes|no) + grounded alternatives_detail = did the paper rule out a NAMED rival
   explanation for this relationship (rule 14).

2. Read the FULL paper: {PAPER_TEXT}. Capture EVERY hypothesis that carries a causal mechanism. Use ONLY
   the paper's own constructs and the directions it states. Every evidence_span MUST be an exact substring;
   if you cannot quote a link's direction exactly, mark that edge "derived": true (sign forced by the other
   two facts) or drop the edge — do not paraphrase a quote into existence.

3. SCOPE: extract the causal claims the paper empirically TESTS — numbered (H1) OR unnumbered predictions it
   tests against data (finance/econ empirical work often isn't numbered; do NOT require a number). Return
   "mechanism_triples": [] ONLY if the paper does not empirically test causal claims at all: a formal-
   modeling/theory paper, a review/meta-analysis, or a qualitative theory-BUILDING case study. Do NOT
   manufacture a hypothesis from model prose. (See spec rule 11.)

Write ONLY the JSON object (schema "mechanism-triples-1.4", paper_id "{PAPER_ID}"). No prose.
